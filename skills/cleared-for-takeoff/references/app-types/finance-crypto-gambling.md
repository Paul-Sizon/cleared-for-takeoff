# Finance, crypto and gambling (3.1.5, 5.1.1(ix), 5.3)

## Finance / banking / lending
- [ ] The developer is the regulated institution, or a licensed partner (5.1.1(ix)). The seller name must match.
- [ ] Personal loans: APR ≤ 36% and no repayment in ≤ 60 days required (3.2.2(ix)). Disclose the maximum APR and terms.
- [ ] Trading: licences for each storefront. Risk disclosures.
- [ ] Account deletion may require extra verification. Explain this in the app (5.1.1(v)).

## Crypto (3.1.5)
- [ ] Wallets: individual developers are fine, but a wallet app must be offered by an organisation.
- [ ] No on-device mining (3.1.5(ii)).
- [ ] Exchanges must be licensed in each storefront, and offered by the exchange itself.
- [ ] ICOs and futures trading only from established banks or securities firms.
- [ ] NFTs: selling NFTs that unlock app features or content must use IAP. Viewing NFTs you own is OK. Buttons to external NFT marketplaces are not allowed outside storefront exceptions.
- [ ] No rewards in crypto for completing tasks (downloading apps, social posts).

## Gambling, betting, lotteries (5.3)
- [ ] Real-money gaming needs the licence, a **geo-restriction to licensed jurisdictions**, must be free on the App Store, and must not use IAP for credits.
- [ ] Brazil: SPA fixed-odds betting licence (May 2026). Brazil age rating is A18.
- [ ] Sweepstakes and contests: official rules in the app, Apple named as not a sponsor, no fee to enter.
- [ ] Age rating reflects gambling (simulated or real).
